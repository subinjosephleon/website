About the Conference

Innovations in
VLSI, Signal Processing & Communication

VISCOM 2027 is organized and hosted by Thiagarajar College of Engineering (TCE), Madurai. The conference provides a premier international forum for researchers, engineers, academic scholars, and industry professionals to deliberate and exchange breakthroughs in the focal themes of VLSI, Intelligence Signal Processing, and Communication systems.

Founded in 1957, Thiagarajar College of Engineering (TCE) is an autonomous, premier institution accredited with NAAC 'A+' grade, celebrated for its rich academic legacy and forward-thinking research culture. Hosting VISCOM 2027 on the vibrant TCE campus in Madurai brings together leading minds to foster collaborative research and next-generation technologies.

The conference spans three days from November 25–27, 2027, featuring plenary keynote lectures, peer-reviewed technical paper presentations, invited talks, and specialized interactive tracks.

Call for Papers

Call for Papers:
Core Themes

VISCOM 2027 invites original, high-quality, unpublished research papers focusing on the key conference themes: VLSI, Intelligence Signal Processing, and Communication. All submissions undergo thorough double-blind peer review by an international technical committee.

Manuscripts should strictly follow standard double-column conference formatting guidelines (up to 6–8 pages). Accepted and presented papers will be submitted for indexing in recognized international repositories.

Publication: Peer-reviewed and accepted papers will be included in the conference proceedings with Scopus indexation support.
Scope Notice: Not limited to the above topics. Relevant multidisciplinary and cross-cutting research submissions are warmly welcome.

  • Low-Power VLSI & Energy Harvesting Architectures
  • SoC, NoC & Multicore Architectures
  • Neuromorphic Hardware & AI Accelerators
  • FPGA-based Prototyping & Embedded Systems
  • VLSI Testing, Verification & Fault Tolerance

  • Deep Learning & Machine Learning for Signals
  • Audio, Speech & Acoustic Signal Processing
  • Biomedical & Healthcare Signal Analytics
  • Image, Video & Multimedia Processing
  • Radar, Sonar & Array Signal Processing
  • Adaptive Filtering, Estimation & Detection
  • Edge AI & Real-Time Signal Processing Systems
  • Statistical & Nonlinear Signal Processing

Theme 3: Next-Generation Communication

  • 5G/6G Wireless Networks & Architectures
  • Massive MIMO, Beamforming & RIS/IRS
  • IoT, Sensor Networks & Edge Computing
  • Optical & Free-Space Optical Communications
  • Satellite & Space Communication Systems
  • Cognitive Radio & Dynamic Spectrum Access
  • Network Security, Quantum Key & Cryptography
  • Vehicle-to-Everything (V2X) Communication

Core Technical Scope

Conference Themes & Tracks

VISCOM 2027 focuses on three foundational and synergistic technical pillars driving modern electronics, artificial intelligence, and global connectivity.

Theme 01

VLSI Systems & Semiconductor Design

Encompassing microelectronics, 3D heterogenous packaging, low-power integrated circuits, neuromorphic hardware accelerators, System-on-Chip (SoC), and cryptographic security circuits.

  • Analog, Digital & Mixed-Signal VLSI
  • Neuromorphic Hardware & AI Accelerators
  • 3D Packaging & Heterogeneous Integration
  • Hardware Security & Fault Tolerant Design
Theme 02

Intelligence Signal Processing

Leveraging artificial intelligence and deep neural representations for acoustic, biomedical, radar, multimedia, and adaptive signal analytics in intelligent edge environments.

  • Deep Learning for Audio & Speech Processing
  • Biomedical & Healthcare Signal Analytics
  • Radar, Sonar & Sensor Array Processing
  • Adaptive Filtering & Edge AI Inference
Theme 03

Next-Generation Communication

Advancing wireless communication frontiers through 5G/6G systems, massive MIMO, IoT infrastructures, satellite connectivity, cognitive radio, and optical network architectures.

  • 5G/6G Wireless Architecture & Massive MIMO
  • IoT, Sensor Networks & Edge Connectivity
  • Optical, Satellite & Space Communications
  • Quantum Key & Secure Network Protocols

Note: Submissions are not limited to the above topics. Authors working across interdisciplinary and cutting-edge frontiers in VLSI, Signal Processing, and Communications are strongly encouraged to submit.
