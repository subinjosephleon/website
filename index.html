About the Conference

India's Premier Forum for
Vision, Graphics & AI Research

The Indian Conference on Computer Vision, Graphics and Image Processing (ICVGIP) is the flagship annual conference of the Indian Unit for Pattern Recognition and Artificial Intelligence (IUPRAI), and one of India's most prestigious venues for disseminating research in Computer Vision, Graphics, Image Processing, Pattern Recognition, and AI.

Established in 1998 and held biennially until 2021, ICVGIP has been organized annually since 2022, reflecting the rapid growth of the research community. The 2026 edition — the 17th — will be jointly hosted by the Indian Statistical Institute, Kolkata and TCG CREST, featuring technical sessions, invited plenary talks, tutorials, and workshops.

Workshops and Tutorials will be held on December 21, 2026, with the main conference running December 22–24, 2026 at IIT Kharagpur Research Park, Kolkata.

Call for Papers

Submit Your
Original Research

ICVGIP 2026 invites original contributions across Computer Vision, Graphics, Image Processing, and allied fields. Submissions must be original, not under simultaneous review, and adhere to the double-blind review policy.

Papers are limited to 8 pages (plus references). Use the official ACM LaTeX template. All submissions via OpenReview.

APC Note: The conference proceedings will be published in the ACM International Conference Proceedings Series (ICPS). APC is likely to be waived for ONOS member institutes in India.
