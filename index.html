About the Conference

A Premier Global Forum for
Visual Computing, Graphics & AI

The International Conference on Visual Computing, Graphics and Image Processing (VISCOM 2026) is proudly organized and hosted by Thiagarajar College of Engineering (TCE), Madurai. VISCOM serves as a high-impact international platform bringing together eminent scholars, pioneering researchers, industry practitioners, and aspiring students from India and around the globe.

Founded in 1957, Thiagarajar College of Engineering (TCE) is an autonomous, premier institution accredited with NAAC 'A+' grade, recognized for its longstanding academic heritage and excellence in technological innovation. Hosting VISCOM 2026 in the historic, culturally vibrant city of Madurai provides an extraordinary backdrop for scholarly dialogue and international collaborations.

Specialized Workshops and Pre-Conference Tutorials will take place on December 21, 2026, followed by the main technical sessions, plenary lectures, and exhibits from December 22–24, 2026 at the serene TCE Madurai campus.

Call for Papers

Submit Your
Original Research

VISCOM 2026 invites original, unpublished research contributions across Visual Computing, Computer Vision, Graphics, Image Processing, and allied AI disciplines. All submissions must follow double-blind peer review protocols.

Full papers are limited to 8 pages (plus references). Typeset your submission using the official ACM LaTeX conference template.

Publication: All accepted and presented papers will be published in the ACM International Conference Proceedings Series (ICPS) and submitted for indexing in Scopus and ACM Digital Library.
