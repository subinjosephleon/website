About the Conference

Innovations in
VLSI, Signal Processing & Communication

IC-VISCOM 2027 is organized and hosted by Thiagarajar College of Engineering (TCE), Madurai. The conference provides a premier international forum for researchers, engineers, academic scholars, and industry professionals to deliberate and exchange breakthroughs in the focal themes of VLSI, Intelligent Signal Processing, and Communication systems.

Founded in 1957, Thiagarajar College of Engineering (TCE) is an autonomous, premier institution accredited with NAAC 'A+' grade, celebrated for its rich academic legacy and forward-thinking research culture. Hosting IC-VISCOM 2027 on the vibrant TCE campus in Madurai brings together leading minds to foster collaborative research and next-generation technologies.

The conference spans across the Second week of December 2027, featuring plenary keynote lectures, peer-reviewed technical paper presentations, pre-conference tutorials, targeted events, hackathons, and interactive technical tracks.

Call for Papers

Call for Papers:
Core Themes

VISCOM 2027 invites original, high-quality, unpublished research papers focusing on the key conference themes: VLSI, Intelligent Signal Processing, and Communication. All submissions undergo thorough double-blind peer review by an international technical committee.

Manuscripts should strictly follow standard double-column conference formatting guidelines (up to 6–8 pages). Accepted and presented papers will be submitted for indexing in recognized international repositories.

Publication: Peer-reviewed and accepted papers will be included in the conference proceedings with Scopus indexation support.
Scope Notice: Not limited to the above topics. Relevant multidisciplinary and cross-cutting research submissions are warmly welcome.

Core Technical Scope

Conference Themes & Tracks

VISCOM 2027 focuses on three foundational and synergistic technical pillars driving modern electronics, artificial intelligence, and global connectivity.

Theme 01

VLSI Systems & Semiconductor Design

Encompassing microelectronics, 3D heterogenous packaging, low-power integrated circuits, neuromorphic hardware accelerators, System-on-Chip (SoC), and cryptographic security circuits.

  • Analog, Digital & Mixed-Signal VLSI
  • Neuromorphic Hardware & AI Accelerators
  • 3D Packaging & Heterogeneous Integration
  • Hardware Security & Fault Tolerant Design
Theme 02

Intelligent Signal Processing

Leveraging artificial intelligence and deep neural representations for acoustic, biomedical, radar, multimedia, and adaptive signal analytics in intelligent edge environments.

  • Deep Learning for Audio & Speech Processing
  • Biomedical & Healthcare Signal Analytics
  • Radar, Sonar & Sensor Array Processing
  • Adaptive Filtering & Edge AI Inference
Theme 03

Next-Generation Communication

Advancing wireless communication frontiers through 5G/6G systems, massive MIMO, IoT infrastructures, satellite connectivity, cognitive radio, and optical network architectures.

  • 5G/6G Wireless Architecture & Massive MIMO
  • IoT, Sensor Networks & Edge Connectivity
  • Optical, Satellite & Space Communications
  • Quantum Key & Secure Network Protocols

Note: Submissions are not limited to the above topics. Authors working across interdisciplinary and cutting-edge frontiers in VLSI, Signal Processing, and Communications are strongly encouraged to submit.

Getting Here

Travel & Transportation

✈️

By Air

Madurai International Airport (IATA: IXM) is approximately 11 km (~20 min) from TCE campus. Direct flights connect from Chennai, Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi, and international hubs.

🚆

By Rail

Madurai Junction Railway Station (MDU) is 8 km from campus. Well-connected by express and superfast trains from all major cities across South India.

🚌

By Road

Madurai is connected by NH 38, NH 44 and NH 85. TCE is on Thiruparankundram Road, accessible by city bus, auto-rickshaw, and cab aggregators (Ola/Uber).

🗺️

Conference Shuttle

Complimentary shuttle service between select hotels and TCE campus on all three conference days (November 25–27, 2027). Schedule shared post-registration.

Explore Madurai

Places to Visit

Madurai — "Athens of the East" — one of India's oldest living cities. Discover magnificent temples, vibrant bazaars, and rich heritage.

🛕

Meenakshi Amman Temple

One of India's grandest ancient temples with towering sculptured gopurams and a 2,500-year-old heritage. Located in the heart of Madurai city (~10 km from TCE).

⛰️

Thiruparankundram Murugan Temple

One of the six abodes of Lord Murugan, carved into a granite hill, just 1 km from TCE campus — ideal for a morning visit during the conference.

🏛️

Thirumalai Nayakar Mahal

A stunning 17th-century Indo-Saracenic palace showcasing Dravidian and Italian architecture with the magnificent Swarga Vilasam.

🏛️

Gandhi Museum

Housed in the historic Tamukkam Palace, the Gandhi Museum displays rare artefacts and photographs from the Indian independence movement.

🎨

Koodal Azhagar Temple

A majestic Vishnu temple with three-storey Dravidian architecture and intricate stone carvings, near the Madurai railway station.

🛍️

Puthu Mandapam & Bazaars

Vibrant street bazaars opposite Meenakshi Temple — famous for silk sarees, handicrafts, jasmine flowers (Madurai Malli), and authentic South Indian street food.
