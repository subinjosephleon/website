About the Conference

Innovations in
VLSI, Signal Processing & Communication

VISCOM 2026 is organized and hosted by Thiagarajar College of Engineering (TCE), Madurai. The conference provides a premier international forum for researchers, engineers, academic scholars, and industry professionals to deliberate and exchange breakthroughs in the focal themes of VLSI, Intelligence Signal Processing, and Communication systems.

Founded in 1957, Thiagarajar College of Engineering (TCE) is an autonomous, premier institution accredited with NAAC 'A+' grade, celebrated for its rich academic legacy and forward-thinking research culture. Hosting VISCOM 2026 on the vibrant TCE campus in Madurai brings together leading minds to foster collaborative research and next-generation technologies.

The conference spans three days from November 25–27, 2026, featuring plenary keynote lectures, peer-reviewed technical paper presentations, invited talks, and specialized interactive tracks.

Call for Papers

Call for Papers:
Core Themes

VISCOM 2026 invites original, high-quality, unpublished research papers focusing on the key conference themes: VLSI, Intelligence Signal Processing, and Communication. All submissions undergo thorough double-blind peer review by an international technical committee.

Manuscripts should strictly follow IEEE/standard double-column formatting guidelines (up to 6–8 pages). Accepted and presented papers will be submitted for indexing in recognized international repositories.

Publication: Peer-reviewed and accepted papers will be included in the conference proceedings with Scopus indexation support.

Theme 1: VLSI Systems & Nanotechnology

  • Analog, Digital & Mixed-Signal VLSI Design
  • Low-Power VLSI & Energy Harvesting Architectures
  • SoC, NoC & Multicore Architectures
  • Neuromorphic Hardware & AI Accelerators
  • FPGA-based Prototyping & Embedded Systems
  • VLSI Testing, Verification & Fault Tolerance
  • Hardware Security & Cryptographic Circuits
  • Emerging Nano-devices & 3D Integrated Circuits

Theme 2: Intelligence Signal Processing

  • Deep Learning & Machine Learning for Signals
  • Audio, Speech & Acoustic Signal Processing
  • Biomedical & Healthcare Signal Analytics
  • Image, Video & Multimedia Processing
  • Radar, Sonar & Array Signal Processing
  • Adaptive Filtering, Estimation & Detection
  • Edge AI & Real-Time Signal Processing Systems
  • Statistical & Nonlinear Signal Processing

Theme 3: Next-Generation Communication

  • 5G/6G Wireless Networks & Architectures
  • Massive MIMO, Beamforming & RIS/IRS
  • IoT, Sensor Networks & Edge Computing
  • Optical & Free-Space Optical Communications
  • Satellite & Space Communication Systems
  • Cognitive Radio & Dynamic Spectrum Access
  • Network Security, Quantum Key & Cryptography
  • Vehicle-to-Everything (V2X) Communication
